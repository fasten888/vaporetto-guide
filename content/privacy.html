Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: this site does not ask who you are, does not set cookies and does not run analytics. It is a set of static pages.

What is collected

Nothing is collected by the site itself. There are no accounts, no contact forms, no comment sections, no advertising scripts and no analytics at the time of publication.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record standard server information — IP address, browser type, requested page — for security and operational purposes. That logging is controlled by GitHub, not by this site, and is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Cookies and local storage

No cookies are set. If a pronunciation button uses your browser's built-in speech engine, the word is spoken locally by your device; no audio is sent to this site or to a third party.

External links

Some pages link to ACTV, GitHub and other external sites. Once you follow a link, that site's privacy policy applies. This site does not control and is not responsible for what those sites collect.
