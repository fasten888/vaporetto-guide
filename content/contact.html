Send a correction, a question or a source.

The fastest route is a public issue on GitHub. It keeps the correction attached to the page it concerns and makes the fix visible to everyone.

Report an error

Open an issue at {{CONTACT_URL}} and include:

  • the page URL;
  • the sentence or figure that is wrong;
  • the correct information, with a source if you have one;
  • the date you checked it.

Fares, route changes and accessibility details are the most useful corrections, because they change most often. A screenshot of the ticket machine or the ACTV notice is ideal.

What to expect

This is a small independent site, not a staffed help desk. Corrections are reviewed when they arrive and substantive fixes are noted in the page's updated date. Questions about a specific trip are best directed to ACTV or your hotel; this site cannot check live disruptions or reserve anything.

Press and permissions

The illustrations on this site are original and may not be reused without permission. Short quotations from the text are fine with a link back to the page.
